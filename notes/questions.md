## Questions worth asking in the first meeting

1. Is the orbital / term / optical / charge-transition distinction now clear enough that I can explain it at the board?
2. Do we agree that the neutral optical assignment here is indirect, and that the silicon hyperfine mismatch should remain visible?
3. Which first small calculation teaches us most: charge thermodynamics, symmetry breaking, or the hyperfine comparison?
4. After the first reproducible results, is a bulk-diamond QE benchmark the right next step, or should we deepen group theory and vibronic reading first?
